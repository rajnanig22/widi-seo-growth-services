What I work on

Research, project management, and sustainability, mostly with community programs. Alongside that, I help organisations with SEO and digital marketing, and keep a small collection of field notes and essays.

now

At Bamboo Village Trust, I lead the delivery of Akademi Bambu Bangli: bamboo dormitory units and a bamboo treatment facility in Bangli, Bali, from design through to handover to the local government. I also support the trust's research on the bamboo value chain, working directly with local producers.

Alongside that, I take on SEO and growth marketing work for a few clients: a research and consulting agency, a dining concept, a hotel group, and a nonprofit that supports at-risk children.

why i write

Some of what I see during fieldwork doesn't fit in a project report: why a village keeps one crop close and pushes another to the slopes, what people trade it for, what they say about it. Writing is how I work through those things. The essays are in Bahasa Indonesia, starting with Yang Bergairah di Landih. They're all on the Writing page.

On the SEO side, I keep SEO notes in English on what I learn from client work, including what a human SEO consultant is still paid for.
