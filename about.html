about

What I work on

Research, project management, and sustainability, mostly with community programs.

background

About eight years in community work: YCAB Foundation, research at Co.Think, BIDUK (an initiative funded by Australia's DFAT), and almost four years with Delterra in Bali. In 2025, I moved into tech to see how digital products grow, and spent a year in AI software companies working on SEO, partnerships, and growth. Now I'm back in community work, at Bamboo Village Trust.

What surprised me is how much carried over. Once, a program almost got rejected because people had been let down before. A woman at a warung kopi told me to just talk normally, not like in a meeting. Later, doing SEO outreach, I wrote that way to a writer in France. The reply was short, but friendly. It reached a real person.

writing

Field notes in Bahasa Indonesia, about things I see at work that don't make it into reports. In English, SEO notes from client work.

outside work

I hike and camp around Bali with my dog Ami. She has climbed Mount Batur and Batukaru, and crossed the sea to Nusa Penida.
