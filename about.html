Now

Why I write

Some of what I see during fieldwork doesn't fit in a project report: why a village keeps one crop close and pushes another to the slopes, what people trade it for, what they say about it. Writing is how I work through those things. The essays are in Bahasa Indonesia, starting with Yang Bergairah di Landih. They're all on the Writing page.

On the SEO side, I keep SEO notes in English on what I learn from client work, including what a human SEO consultant is still paid for.
