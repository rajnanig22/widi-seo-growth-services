Getting Found

ItemWhat it includesResult
Technical SEO audits Fixing what's blocking indexing or ranking: canonical tags, crawl errors, duplicate metadata, slow load times. Pages that were invisible to Google start showing up in search.
Keyword research Finding the words people actually type, not the words an organisation uses internally. Content built around real searches, not guesses.
On-page optimisation Title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking, URL structure. A page that's easy for people and search engines to read at a glance.
Competitor research Checking what's already ranking, and where the real gap is. A short, specific list of openings, not a vague idea to "do more content."
